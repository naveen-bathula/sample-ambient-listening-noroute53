# Ambient Clinical Documentation with Amazon Connect Health

Welcome! In this workshop you will deploy and use an end-to-end **ambient
clinical documentation** application. You will play the role of a clinician:
pull up a patient record, stream a clinical conversation to
[Amazon Connect Health](https://aws.amazon.com/connect/health/), watch a
real-time transcript build with speaker labels, and review an AI-generated
structured SOAP note before writing it back to the patient's chart in
[OpenEMR](https://www.open-emr.org/).

## What you will build

By the end of this workshop you will have a running environment with:

- **OpenEMR** on Amazon ECS Fargate acting as the electronic health record (EHR),
  loaded with synthetic patients.
- A **Next.js demo application** on ECS Fargate that streams audio to Amazon
  Connect Health and displays live transcription and SOAP notes.
- A complete **clinician workflow**: patient selection → ambient audio capture →
  live transcript → SOAP note review → write-back to the EHR.

![Architecture](/static/images/architecture.png)

## What you will learn

- How Amazon Connect Health Ambient Listening turns a clinical conversation into
  a structured note.
- How real-time transcription with speaker diarization works.
- How AI-generated clinical documentation fits a **human-in-the-loop** review
  workflow before it reaches the patient record.
- How the pieces connect: browser → load balancer → backend → FHIR API +
  Connect Health → clinical note write-back.

## Who this is for

Solutions architects, developers, and technical practitioners in healthcare who
want hands-on experience with ambient clinical documentation on AWS. No prior
Connect Health experience is required.

{{% notice warning %}}
This workshop uses **synthetic patient data only** (generated by Synthea). Never
use real protected health information (PHI) in this environment. A Business
Associate Agreement (BAA) with AWS is required before using these services with
real PHI in production.
{{% /notice %}}

## How to move through the workshop

Work through the modules in order using the navigation on the left:

1. **Introduction** – what ambient documentation is and how the app is built.
2. **Prerequisites & Setup** – confirm which account path you are on and your tools.
3. **Access Your Environment** – in a provided account it is already deployed; in
   your own account you deploy it with one command.
4. **Explore the EHR** – log in to OpenEMR, find your patient, and confirm the
   environment is healthy.
5. **Create Your Connect Health Domain** – create the `ambient-workshop` domain
   the app uses for transcription.
6. **Run Ambient Documentation** – stream a conversation and watch it transcribe.
7. **Review the SOAP Note** – edit, approve, and write back to the chart.
8. **Clean Up** – tear everything down so you stop incurring cost.
