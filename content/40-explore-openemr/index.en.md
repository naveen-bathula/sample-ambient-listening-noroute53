# Explore the EHR

Before running the ambient documentation flow, take a moment to see the patient
record the way a clinician would — in OpenEMR, the electronic health record.

## Open OpenEMR

1. Open the **OpenEMR URL** from the deployment summary in your browser.
2. Accept the self-signed certificate warning (**Advanced → Proceed**).
3. Log in with the OpenEMR administrator credentials.

{{% notice info %}}
The OpenEMR admin credentials are stored in **AWS Secrets Manager** (created by
the OpenEMR stack). Retrieve them from the console under
**Secrets Manager**, or ask your facilitator. Never hard-code or share these
credentials outside the workshop.
{{% /notice %}}

## Find the demo patient

The deployment loaded 100 synthetic patients, including a dedicated demo patient.

1. Use the patient search in OpenEMR.
2. Search for **Margaret Smith**.
3. Open her chart and look around: demographics, existing encounters, and
   clinical history are all synthetic data generated by Synthea.

Take note of what the chart looks like **now**. After you run the ambient
documentation flow and approve the note, you will come back here and see a **new
encounter note** written back to this same record.

## Why this matters

This step shows the "before" state. The value of ambient documentation is that
the clinician can have a natural conversation with the patient and end up with a
structured note in this record — without manually typing it. In the next module
you will produce exactly that.

Continue to **Run Ambient Documentation**.
